Payroll workflow

Rules and monthly steps from the payroll Read Me.

Payroll period
SQLite stores the current month and derives its calendar-day count.
Attendance & loss of pay
Work days, leave days, longest spell, process allowance, punctual incentive and full-attendance bonus are monthly inputs.
Leave usage
Casual leave is used before earned leave; after three continuous leave days, day four onward uses earned leave.
LOP calculation
Unused leave after CL and EL balances becomes LOP; LOP amount is LOP days × Basic ÷ 30.
Statutory review
PF, ESI, bonus, gratuity and TDS estimates use imported settings. Verify applicable law before filing or disbursing.
Payroll payment date
Salary is scheduled for the fifth of the following month. This portal does not initiate payment.
ESI review windows
April–September and October–March.
Month close
Archive the payroll to preserve history and reset the current month's leave inputs.
