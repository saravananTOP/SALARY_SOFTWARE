Workbook payroll workflow

Review-only guidance based on the source workbook instructions.

Payroll period
Settings controls the month and calendar-day count used by formulas.
Attendance & loss of pay
Monthly work, leave, continuous-spell, incentive and late inputs feed the workbook's LOP and deduction formulas.
Leave usage
Casual leave is used before earned leave; after three continuous leave days, day four onward uses earned leave.
LOP calculation
Loss-of-pay amount follows the workbook formula: LOP days × Basic ÷ 30.
Statutory review
PF, ESI, bonus, gratuity and TDS are presented from their separate workbook registers; verify applicable law and source formulas.
Payroll payment date
The workbook guidance schedules salary payment for the fifth of the following month. This portal does not initiate payment.
ESI review windows
April–September and October–March.
Month close
Archiving and monthly resets remain manual workbook-owner actions and are not performed by this portal.
